FAQs
Frequently asked questions
What is CommandoHSC?
CommandoHSC is an AI-powered tool built exclusively for NSW HSC students. It analyses your written exam responses, colour-codes command terms, estimates your mark range, and gives you structured marker feedback — in seconds.
Is the first analysis really free?
Yes. Sign up with your email and you get 3 free analyses — no credit card required.
Do I need a credit card to sign up?
No. You can create an account and use your 3 free analyses with just your email address. A credit card is only needed when you choose to purchase credits.
What do I need to get started?
Just your email address and an exam response you'd like to analyse. You can upload a photo, PDF, Word document, or paste plain text directly.
What file types can I upload?
You can submit your response as a photo, PDF, Word document, or plain text — whatever is easiest for you.
How long does an analysis take?
Analyses are generated in seconds. Once you submit your response, feedback is returned almost immediately.
What does the feedback actually look like?
You receive a colour-coded breakdown of your response showing how well you've addressed the command term, an estimated mark range, and structured feedback across four panels — strengths, gaps, and improvements. Everything is available as a downloadable PDF.
Can I use it with my school assessments, not just past HSC papers?
Yes. CommandoHSC works with any written exam response — past HSC papers, trial exams, school assessments, or teacher-written tasks. Just paste or upload your response and the question.
How accurate is the mark estimate?
The estimated mark range is calibrated to the NSW Performance Band Indicators — the same criteria your markers use. It is an AI-estimated projection, not a guaranteed HSC mark. Always discuss feedback with your teacher.
Is the feedback the same as what a real teacher would give?
CommandoHSC was built by an experienced PDHPE teacher with over a decade of HSC classroom experience, so the feedback is structured the way real teachers frame it — across four panels covering strengths, gaps, and improvements. It's a study tool designed to complement your teacher, not replace them.
Will the AI feedback match what NESA markers look for?
The feedback is calibrated to NESA's Performance Band Indicators and uses the same band language official markers use. It's the closest AI feedback gets to real marking criteria — but always discuss your results with your teacher.
Do my credits expire?
Yes — credits expire two years from the date of purchase. Buy when you need them and use them at your own pace.
Can I get a refund if I'm not happy?
Credits that have been used on analyses are non-refundable, as the AI service is delivered immediately upon use. Unused credits may be refunded at our discretion if requested within 30 days of purchase, provided no credits from that purchase have been spent.
What happens to my credits if I don't use them all?
Your credits stay in your account and are valid for two years from the date of purchase. There are no monthly fees or subscriptions — buy once and use them whenever you need them.
Can I share my credits with someone else?
No. Credits are non-transferable between accounts. Each account is for individual use only.
Which subjects does CommandoHSC cover?
CommandoHSC is currently built for HSC Health and Movement Science. Additional subjects are coming in Term 3 2026.
When are more subjects coming?
More subjects are launching in Term 3 2026. Follow us on Instagram or TikTok to be the first to know when they go live.
Is my exam response stored or shared?
Your responses are processed to generate feedback and stored securely. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties except as required to deliver the service. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Is my data used to train AI?
No. Anthropic, our AI provider, does not use API data to train their models by default. Your responses are used only to generate your feedback.

01Who we are

CommandoHSC is an AI-powered HSC exam response analyser run by a teacher/developer in New South Wales, Australia. This policy covers how we handle your personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024 (Cth). Questions? hello@commandohsc.com

02What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the Service — nothing more.

  • Account — email address, and name/profile picture if you sign in with Google. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.
  • Exam responses — the text you submit is sent to Anthropic's Claude API and not stored on our servers after analysis.
  • Payments — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Usage data — credit balance, subject and question type per analysis, date/time of analyses, and basic browser/device info (via cookieless analytics).

We don't collect sensitive information (health, racial, religious, biometric data) and don't solicit it.

03How we use it
  • Deliver AI feedback on your submitted responses
  • Manage your account, credits, and purchases
  • Send transactional emails (receipts, confirmations, low-credit alerts)
  • Monitor reliability and fix errors
  • Detect and prevent abuse
  • Meet our legal obligations

We don't sell your data, use it for marketing, or use your exam responses to train AI models.

04AI and automated decisions

Your submitted text is processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate feedback and estimated mark ranges — without human review of individual responses. This is disclosed in line with Australian automated decision-making transparency obligations (APP 1 and the forthcoming requirements under the Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024, commencing December 2026).

AI feedback is a study aid only — not official NESA assessment. Estimated marks are indicative. Anthropic does not use API data to train their models by default. You must not include your full name, student number, or school name in submitted responses — this information is not required for analysis and including it unnecessarily increases your privacy exposure.
05Third parties and overseas disclosure

For overseas providers, we take reasonable steps to ensure they handle your data consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles. We may also disclose information where required by Australian law.

06How long we keep it

  • Transaction records — 7 years (financial record-keeping obligations)
  • Technical logs — up to 90 days, then purged
